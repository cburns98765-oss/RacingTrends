Each-way value

Enter the Betfair win price and the bookmaker's price for each runner. The page works out each horse's fair chance of winning and placing, and whether the bookmaker's each-way bet is worth more than it costs. Extra-place offers are where the value usually is.

Prices can be fractional (5/1, 11-4, evs) or decimal (6.0). Tick NR for non-runners. With no Betfair price the bookie price is used for that horse's chance. Value = expected profit per £1 staked (an each-way bet of £1 costs £2), ignoring Rule 4 deductions and dead heats.

Rules of thumb (tested on 2024–26)

For runners between 2/1 and 20/1, the share of the Betfair SP a bookmaker's price needs to be for the each-way bet to break even, and what each-way bets would actually have returned in 2024–26 if struck at Betfair SP. Fair chances come from Betfair SP, checked against 375,000 real results.

Below 100% means the bet is fair even when the bookmaker is shorter than the exchange. Standard terms are roughly break-even, except 16+ runner handicaps (4 places at 1/4). One extra place adds about 15–25%, which is enough to beat most bookmaker margins. Bookmakers limit accounts that only take these bets.
