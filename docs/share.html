Race graphics

Pick a race to get a five-slide carousel for Instagram (1080 × 1350): the race, its key trends, past winners, the pace map and the best fits on the trends. Everything comes straight from the dashboard data, so nothing is made up. Download the slides as images, or export the JSON for the carousel app.

"Best fits on the trends" are runners matching the profile of past winners. They are trends, not tips: backtests show the market already prices them in.
